# Strava → Gevety

Strava sends each activity to Gevety the moment you save it, so it is the
quickest way to get runs, rides, swims and watch workouts into your Activities.

## What you need

- A Strava account and the Strava app: [strava.com/mobile](https://www.strava.com/mobile)
  (iPhone, Android, and an Apple Watch app that records directly from the wrist).
- A Gevety account at [gevety.com](https://gevety.com).

## Set it up

1. In Gevety go to **Profile → Sources → Strava** and press **Connect with Strava**.
   Approve the permissions on Strava. That is the whole connection.
2. Record activities with the Strava app on your phone or watch, or let your
   device upload to Strava (Garmin, Wahoo and most watches can do this).
3. Each activity Strava finalizes appears in Gevety within a minute. Existing
   history is pulled on connect.

## Apple Watch users

- The Strava watch app records workouts straight to Strava, with heart rate.
- If you prefer Apple's own Workout app, a third-party sync app such as
  HealthFit can forward those sessions to Strava. This is optional: connecting
  Apple Health to Gevety directly (see [apple-health](../apple-health/)) already
  brings those workouts in, together with sleep and HRV, which Strava never carries.
- If both Strava and Apple Health send the same session, Gevety keeps one
  record and merges the details.

Gevety shows Strava data with the "Powered by Strava" mark as Strava's
guidelines require. Gevety is not affiliated with Strava.
